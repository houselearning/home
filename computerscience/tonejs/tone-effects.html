🎧 Tone.js Effects

Tone.js offers a variety of built-in audio effects that you can use to enhance your sounds. These include Reverb, Delay, Distortion, Chorus, and more. Effects can be chained between instruments and speakers.

🔥 Common Effects in Tone.js

  • Reverb – Simulates space and room reflections
  • FeedbackDelay – Echo with feedback
  • Distortion – Adds grit and saturation
  • Chorus – Modulated delay for lush sounds
  • Phaser – Swirly sci-fi phase shifting
  • AutoFilter – Automatic filter modulation
  • PingPongDelay – Stereo delay with left/right alternation

⚠️ Tip: Chaining Effects

You can chain multiple effects together using `.connect()` like this:


synth.connect(distortion).connect(reverb).toDestination();
