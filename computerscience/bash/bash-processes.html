⚙️ Bash Processes

In Bash, processes are running programs. You can list, manage, and control processes using commands.

📚 Common Process Commands

  • ps — Lists your running processes
  • top — Real-time process monitor
  • kill [PID] — Sends a signal to terminate a process by its PID
  • jobs — Lists background jobs in your shell
  • fg %job_number — Brings a background job to the foreground
  • bg %job_number — Resumes a suspended job in the background
