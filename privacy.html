Privacy Information

HouseLearning provides free educational lessons and games. This page explains the major privacy choices currently associated with the public website; it should be reviewed before advertising or a change in data collection is enabled.

Information you provide

Signed-in account features may process account identifiers and authentication information through Firebase. Signed-in learner progress may include lesson activity and practice results. Do not enter passwords or sensitive personal information into lesson or feedback fields.

Cookies and analytics

HouseLearning uses consent controls for analytics and advertising storage. Analytics consent defaults are denied until a visitor makes a choice in the cookie banner. The site uses Google Analytics measurement and may use browser storage for preferences and saved progress.

Advertising

HouseLearning is preparing for possible future advertising, but advertisements are not approved or enabled solely by this page. Before enabling advertising, HouseLearning must review applicable child-directed rules, publisher policies, consent requirements, and the placement of ads for K–12 audiences.

Your choices

Use the cookie preferences control on the website to review consent choices where available. Signed-in users can manage account access through the authentication pages. Contact the maintainers for questions about data deletion or account privacy.

Third-party services

Some lessons, games, videos, and authentication features load resources from third-party services. Each service may receive ordinary technical data such as an IP address or browser information when its content is requested.

Updates

This page is informational and will need periodic review as analytics, advertising, authentication, or student tools change.
